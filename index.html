What is the difference between coworking and a virtual office?

Coworking gives you a desk or cabin to work from. A virtual office gives you a professional business address and mail handling, without a physical workspace.

Can I book a meeting room for just a few hours?

Yes. Our meeting and conference rooms are available by the hour, so you pay only for the time you use.

How do I book a tour?
